### About

Most of my work is the unglamorous half of applied AI: taking a process that currently lives in WhatsApp threads, spreadsheets, and one employee's head, and turning it into something a machine can run reliably. The hard part is rarely which model to call — it's the retrieval, the orchestration, the failure modes, and the integration work that decides whether any of it survives contact with production.

- **Career intelligence** — a job matching and skill-gap analysis engine, integrated with India's National Career Service (Ministry of Labour & Employment). Deployed with university placement cells.
- **[Zobique Labs](https://labs.zobique.com)** — AI automation and custom software for businesses: lead qualification pipelines, voice agents, internal tooling, workflow systems.
- **Vertical AI products** — built from problems that show up repeatedly across client work.
- Reach me at **[ceo@zobique.com](mailto:ceo@zobique.com)**

### Selected Work

| Project | What it is |
|---|---|
| [AI-Telecalling](https://github.com/Risabkshetri/AI-Telecalling) | Voice agent for real-estate lead qualification — objection handling and viewing bookings, over browser or phone, in English + 10 Indian languages |
| [ventureai](https://github.com/Risabkshetri/ventureai) | Multi-agent system for analyzing and validating startup ideas, with a verification layer over model output |
| [ProposalgenAI](https://github.com/Risabkshetri/ProposalgenAI) | Turns raw client notes into print-ready PDF proposals — Next.js, FastAPI, Gemini, WeasyPrint |
| [PortfolioGen](https://github.com/Risabkshetri/PortfolioGen-frontend) | No-code portfolio builder with real-time preview ([backend](https://github.com/Risabkshetri/PortfolioGen-Backend)) |

Some of the production work — the matching engine and most client deployments — isn't public. Happy to walk through the architecture if it's relevant to what you're building.
